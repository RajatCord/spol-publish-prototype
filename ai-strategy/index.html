SPOL · AI strategy · 23 Oct 2026

AI included, priced in credits, run by SPOL

Every SPOL module carries an annual allowance of AI credits for the whole institution. Users see what an AI action costs before they run it; they never choose a model, a provider or a setting. SPOL routes each action to the right model, absorbs the cost risk, and keeps the price as it is today: one price per module, as many users as the institution wants.

Seven principles

Everything below follows from these. If a later decision contradicts one, the principle wins.

Included, not sold separately

AI is part of every module. There is no AI tier, no AI seat and no per-user charge. Adding users never costs more.

One unit, one number

Credits only. Never tokens, never dollars on a working screen, never two meters.

The cost is on the button

Every paid action shows its credits before it runs. Small helpers are free and say nothing.

SPOL chooses the model

No model picker, no "fast or thorough" switch, no bring-your-own key. SPOL routes, pins, tests and pays.

Never more than the quote

A failed run costs nothing; an unfinished one is refunded; a stored result is free to view again.

No surprise at the deadline

Work in the two weeks before a submission is never stopped by the allowance, and grace use is never billed.

Suggest, never act

AI output changes nothing until a person accepts it. Every AI action is recorded and can be disclosed to an accreditor.

What the market does

Accreditation vendors do not publish AI pricing. General SaaS has converged on a predictable base fee with an AI allowance inside it, metered in credits, with packs above it. Pure per-action billing and paid AI seats are the shapes buyers resist.

The credit

What it is

  • One credit is worth $0.10 at pack prices. That is the only conversion anyone outside SPOL ever needs, and it appears only on the coordinator's purchasing screen.
  • Weights are small whole numbers, fixed for the contract term, printed on the action, scaled by size where the work scales.
  • One pool per institution, shared by every user and every module, renewed at the start of the institution's own fiscal year; what is left at year end expires.
  • Quoted before, charged after. The quote places a hold; the charge is never above it; a failure releases it.
  • Stored results are free. Re-running AI readiness charges only standards whose text or evidence changed; a re-run caused by SPOL changing a model or prompt is free.

What is always free

  • Spelling, grammar, punctuation and style, as you type and at readiness.
  • Ask SPOL, the product help.
  • The thirteen readiness checks, send back, comments, reminders, publishing in every format.
  • Improve wording, Suggest support, Privacy check and Alt text. They cost SPOL a fraction of a cent, and accessibility and privacy work should never be rationed.
  • Viewing any stored AI result, by anyone in the institution.

Expiry and the accreditation cycle

Credits belong to a credit year, the institution's own fiscal year within the contract term. Unused credits, included and purchased alike, expire at the end of the credit year. Nothing carries into the next year, so every year starts from the same allowance and the balance always means one thing. Accreditation work still comes in waves, so the allowance is sized to cover a full self-study year on its own, and a self-study year that needs more is planned ahead with a pack quoted as a fixed line item. The renewal date and the expiry date are the same, and the coordinator is reminded 60 and 30 days before.

Pricing

One price per module for unlimited users stays. AI adds an annual allowance to each module, a modest lift in list price to carry it, and packs for institutions that use more. Credits are for the year they are issued in.

Trust, data and the accreditor

Drafting is opt-in

Draft the response and Draft a reply are off until a coordinator turns them on for a module. AI text is labelled "AI draft, not yet reviewed" until a person edits or accepts it.

Ready to disclose

The AI-involvement report exports as a disclosure appendix: what AI suggested, what a person accepted, per standard. It supports institutions answering accreditor guidance on AI, such as SACSCOC's December 2024 statement, which keeps responsibility for accuracy with the institution.

Usage is not surveillance

Usage is reported by role and unit. Person-level detail is visible only to named AI admins, kept 90 days, never shows prompt text, and by contract is never used in personnel evaluation.

One data statement

The provider keeps nothing and trains on nothing. SPOL's logs hold metadata only. AI results the user keeps live in the institution's own records under its retention rules. Original files are never sent; document checks send extracted text with names tokenised.

FERPA

SPOL acts as a school official. Student work in the Assessment module is excluded from AI or de-identified before it is sent, and that quality is tested after de-identification.

Opt-outs

An institution switch for all AI, a switch for narrative drafting, and a per-section opt-out a writer can set on their own work.

What SPOL takes care of

The promise that users never think about models is only true if this works every day.

AreaHow SPOL runs it
RoutingA routing table, one row per action: model and version, reasoning effort, output cap, task budget, prompt version, size bands. Light actions run at low reasoning effort. The table is versioned and frozen for an institution's report until that report is submitted.
LedgerA quote places a hold keyed by an invocation id the client creates. Automatic retries and route failover reuse the same key, so they never charge twice. Balance, monthly limits and grace are checked in one transaction, so concurrent users cannot overdraw the pool. A cancelled stream is charged once half of its output has been shown.
Cost controlInput caps per action, splitting above a size band, prompt caching of the report across standards, batch pricing for scheduled work only. An internal spend limit per run stops and refunds rather than overruns.
Model changesA golden set per action and per accreditor (citation precision at least 95 %, no invented evidence, readiness flags changing on fewer than 5 % of unchanged standards); two weeks in shadow, a 10 % canary, one-step rollback. A model retirement is planned as a migration months ahead, never discovered mid-cycle.
RoutesClaude models from Anthropic through more than one cloud. Failover goes only to routes in contracted regions with zero retention confirmed; otherwise the action stops. Every route is a named sub-processor. Failover is tested every quarter.
TelemetryEvery call records tenant, action, model, prompt version, route, tokens by kind, stop reason, retries and cost against a versioned price table. Reconciled daily with provider usage reports and cloud bills to within 2 %. A tenant alert fires when spend passes five times its seven-day baseline.
FreshnessA stored result is keyed to the input, the model and the prompt version, so a change to any of them makes it out of date rather than silently stale. Truncated output is detected and shown as incomplete, never as finished.
PeaksSubmission dates cluster. Capacity is reserved ahead of known deadlines, long runs queue with an estimated time, and off-peak work moves to scheduled batch.

Finance and contract

Revenue recognition

  • Included credits are part of the subscription and recognised over the term.
  • Purchased credits are deferred and recognised as used; unused credits are recognised as breakage at the end of the credit year in which they expire, under a written policy.
  • Pro-rata refund of purchased credits at termination, as some state rules require.

Margin controls

  • Fully loaded AI cost of goods: model fees, infrastructure, telemetry, the language-check licence, support and cloud route premiums.
  • Target AI cost below 3 % of contract value; a review is triggered above it.
  • Pack margin at least 55 % at worst-case cost.

Contract package

  • An AI addendum: provider, US processing, zero retention on every route, no training, sub-processors with 30 days' notice, model change log.
  • FERPA school-official terms; state contract riders.
  • Allowance, year-end expiry, grace, first-year promise and weight protection written in.
  • Pack prices fixed for the term; AI reserve against a purchase order.
  • No use of usage data in personnel evaluation.

Rollout

  1. Pilot. Six to eight institutions across sizes and cycle stages, on Accreditation, allowance on, packs off, drafting opt-in. Measure credits per institution per month at p50, p90 and p99, tokens per action with reasoning, regeneration rates and refusals. Exit when twelve weeks of data are in and every action meets the 60 % rule at p90.
  2. Set the numbers. Weights and the allowance formula fixed from pilot data; the 8 % list lift approved with sales against real competitor quotes; contract package and AI addendum signed off by legal.
  3. General availability. Allowance in every module; packs and the AI reserve on; the coordinator console, alerts, ledger export and AI-involvement report live.
  4. Consortia and multi-year. Shared packs for systems, weight protection in multi-year terms, the year-end summary of what AI did.
