What AI can do at Westmoor

Turn a capability off and it disappears for everyone; nothing already written or decided is changed.

AI drafting of narrativesDraft the response, Draft a reply to feedback. Writers get a first draft to edit; every AI draft is labelled until a person edits or accepts it. Off by default; some accreditors and boards ask for this to stay off.
