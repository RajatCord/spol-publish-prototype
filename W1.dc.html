Hall, Tracy Compliance50%
NarrativeSACSCOC-2018Institutional
Standard statement
The institution has a clearly defined, comprehensive, and published mission specific to the institution and appropriate for higher education. The mission addresses teaching and learning and, where applicable, research and public service.
Narrative response

Westmoor University is committed to helping people improve their lives through education. Since its founding in 1952 the goals by responding to the needs of the community, and the mission is published in the University Catalog and on the public website.

The Mission Statement Review Committee periodically reviews and updates the mission statement with input from faculty, staff, administrators and student leaders. Based on the committee's review and the comparison of mission statements of all types of colleges and universities across the nation, considering purpose, values, strategic themes and goals [2], on March 20, 2024 the committee presented the revised statement to the District Board of Trustees. After discussion, the new mission statement was approved and recorded in the Board Minutes [22].

The revised mission statement is shown on the following page. The University publishes it in the following institutional publications:

  • University Catalog
  • Student Handbook
